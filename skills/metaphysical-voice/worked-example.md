# Worked Example: The Skill Applied End-to-End

This walks through producing one short piece from a vague request, showing each decision point from the main SKILL.md and references actually firing. Use this as a model for how to move through the skill, not as content to imitate verbatim.

---

## The request

> "Write something like a manifesto for a group of artists who think AI-generated art is killing creativity, but make it feel ancient and almost religious, not like a normal angry essay."

## Step 1 — The four calibrations

1. **Source of authority**: Collective will (this is explicitly a manifesto for a group) — but the user wants it inflected with religious/ancient weight, so: manifesto grammar (Register 1) as the base, borrowing prophetic/scriptural cadence (Register 3) for texture, not a pure gnostic or aphoristic voice.
2. **Relationship to reader**: Commanded and recruited — manifestos want adherents — but with an implied "you" cast partly as the old order (the AI-generated-art world) being superseded.
3. **Degree of system**: Light invented cosmology is worth adding — one or two unexplained terms for "real" art vs. the generated kind — rather than pure atmosphere, since it'll make the piece reread-able and give it texture beyond raw anger.
4. **Length/structure**: Numbered Declaration Sequence (Structure 1) — manifesto-native, and numbering performs the confidence the user wants instead of "a normal angry essay."

**State the read:** *"Reading this as: a manifesto voice with prophetic/scriptural cadence layered in, light invented terminology for 'true making' vs. the hollow kind, structured as a numbered declaration sequence, roughly 7-9 points."*

## Step 2 — Draft with devices deliberately placed

Planning before writing:
- Open short and declarative (Structure 1's build pattern).
- Plant one invented term early without defining it ("the Unmaking" — for what happens to a piece of art when a machine generates it instead of a hand), then reuse it once more later (the cosmology-reuse technique in `structures.md`).
- Use anaphora once for a build (point 4 or 5), asyndeton once for urgency, one real paradox, one apophasis moment.
- End on the longest, heaviest point, echoing the opening's phrasing with a twist (Structure 1's closure pattern).
- Sentence-final stress: check every line ends on its heaviest word, not a trailing qualifier.

## Step 3 — The piece

> **On the Unmaking**
>
> I. We did not lose our hands. We forgot what they were for.
>
> II. A machine can finish a painting. It cannot want one. Wanting is the part that was never optional.
>
> III. They call it creation because the word was sitting there, unguarded, and theft is easier when the door is already open.
>
> IV. We have watched the gallery fill with images that took no time, cost no doubt, and changed no one — least of all the hand that triggered them, because there was no hand, only a request.
>
> V. This is the Unmaking: not the destruction of art, but its replacement with something that resembles art the way a photograph of fire resembles warmth.
>
> VI. We do not fear the machine. We fear what we will accept once we are tired enough.
>
> VII. To make badly, with your own hand, trembling, is worth more than to generate perfectly with none — because the trembling is the only part of the work that was actually yours to give.
>
> VIII. The Unmaking does not announce itself. It arrives disguised as convenience, and convenience has never once in its history knocked before entering.
>
> IX. We did not lose our hands. We are using them now to take them back.

## Step 4 — Pass against the failure-modes checklist

1. **Stock vocabulary** — scanned for veil/threshold/ancient/sacred/cosmic/void cluster: none present. Pass.
2. **Adjective inflation** — checked every noun for 2+ stacked adjectives: none found (the piece relies on images and structure, not modifiers). Pass.
3. **"We" specificity** — the excluded party is explicit and concrete throughout (those who use/accept generated art, the gallery, "we" who are "tired enough" to accept it) — not a vague cosmic "we." Pass.
4. **Tricolon escalation** (point IV) — "took no time, cost no doubt, and changed no one" — escalates from practical to emotional to relational, third item is the heaviest. Pass.
5. **Paradox test** (point VII) — claim: trembling, imperfect work has more value than perfect generated work. Plain-sentence test: "Effort and risk are what make something meaningfully yours, even if the result is worse." Real underlying truth exists — paradox passes.
6. **Mystery left unexplained** — "the Unmaking" is named in the title and point I implicitly, given one concrete definition-by-simile in point V (photograph of fire / warmth), then reused in point VIII without re-explaining — not over-resolved. Pass.
7. **Scale anchored to specifics** — the cosmic-feeling claims (point VI, point VIII) are anchored to a small concrete moment (a gallery, a hand, "tired enough") rather than floating at pure abstraction. Pass.
8. **Register consistency** — manifesto grammar (collective "we," declarative present-as-fact) sustained throughout; the one scriptural-cadence borrowing (parataxis in point VIII: "It arrives disguised as convenience, and convenience has never once...") is a deliberate, contained texture-shift, not drift. Pass.
9. **Cuttable by a third?** — checked; nine points already each carry distinct content with no redundant pair. Kept at nine.
10. **Briefing tic (#15)** — scanned for identity punches, "that's the tell/gist/whole thing," "full stop," and any opener that explains the document. None present. Short lines here are numbered declarations (Structure 1), not captions on a research pile. Pass.

## What to notice about the process

The four-calibration step took thirty seconds of thinking and prevented an entire wrong draft (e.g., writing this as a pure aphoristic-philosophical piece, or a sustained single-paragraph invocation, either of which the user's actual request didn't call for). The device-planning step before drafting — deciding *where* anaphora, asyndeton, and paradox would go — produced a more varied texture than writing intuitively and hoping devices would emerge. The failure-modes pass at the end caught nothing major here because the planning was deliberate, which is the point: most of the failure modes in that file are symptoms of *not* planning structure and concreteness up front, not things you fix reliably after the fact.
